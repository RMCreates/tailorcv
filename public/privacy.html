Privacy policy

The short version: we use what you paste only to write your documents, card details go to Stripe and never to us, and we delete orders after 30 days.

Effective date: [DATE]

Who we are

TailorCV ("we", "us") is a pay-per-use service that rewrites a resume for a specific job posting and writes a matching cover letter and match summary. You can contact us about privacy at support@YOURDOMAIN.com.

What we collect

  • Your resume text. Whatever you paste into the resume box. Resumes usually contain personal information such as your name, contact details, work history and education, so only include what you're comfortable sharing.
  • The job posting text you paste.
  • Your email address, if you choose to give it. It's optional and used for your receipt and, if needed, to contact you about your order.
  • The documents we generate for you (the tailored resume, cover letter and match summary).
  • Payment status from Stripe, such as whether your checkout was completed and its Stripe session ID.
  • Basic technical data that web servers routinely log, such as IP address, browser type and request times, used for security and troubleshooting.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe through Stripe Checkout. You enter your card details on Stripe's page, not ours. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe's handling of your payment data is covered by Stripe's privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To generate your tailored resume, cover letter and match summary.
  • To show you your results and let you retry if generation fails.
  • To confirm payment, handle refunds and answer support requests.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your resume to advertise to you.

AI processing by Anthropic

To write your documents, we send your resume text and the job posting to Anthropic's API, which runs the Claude AI model. Anthropic processes this text to produce the output and handles it under its own commercial terms and privacy policy. We don't send your payment details to Anthropic.

How long we keep it

Orders, including your resume text, the job posting, your email and the generated documents, are deleted 30 days after they're created. Save a copy of your results before then. Stripe keeps payment records as required for its own legal and accounting obligations, and server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run TailorCV: Stripe (payments), Anthropic (AI generation) and our hosting provider. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to delete your order before the 30 days are up, or ask what information we hold about you, by emailing support@YOURDOMAIN.com with your order link or the email you used. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising cookies. The order page saves what you've typed in your browser's session storage so you don't lose it if you leave and come back during checkout; it's cleared when you close the tab.

Children

TailorCV isn't intended for children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
