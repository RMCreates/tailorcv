How to tailor a social media manager resume to the job posting

Social media hiring managers can see your work publicly, so your resume should point to it and explain the strategy and results behind it. They want someone who can plan, create, publish, engage and report, not just post.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

ATS filters often look for platform names (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, Facebook, Pinterest), tools (Sprout Social, Hootsuite, Later, Buffer, Meta Business Suite, Canva), content creation, community management, paid social, influencer partnerships, and analytics. Use the platforms the posting emphasizes.

Hiring managers look for the brands you managed, audience growth, engagement and conversion results, and evidence that your content served business goals. Include a link to accounts or a portfolio of posts. Make clear whether you also created content (copy, short-form video, design) or managed creators and agencies.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • Social media strategy
  • Content calendar
  • Instagram / TikTok / LinkedIn
  • Community management
  • Short-form video
  • Paid social
  • Influencer partnerships
  • Sprout Social / Hootsuite
  • Social analytics and reporting
  • Brand voice

Example bullet points

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Managed Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn for a skincare brand, planning a monthly content calendar and producing short-form videos in-house.
  • Example: Grew the brand's TikTok account from launch to 50k followers in a year with a recurring educational series.
  • Example: Set up a monthly social report linking campaign posts to site traffic and promo-code sales, used by the marketing lead to plan budgets.

Common mistakes on social media manager resumes

  • Reporting follower counts with no engagement or business results.
  • Not linking to any accounts or examples.
  • Listing every platform when you only have real depth in two or three.
