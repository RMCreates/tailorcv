How to tailor a customer service representative resume to the job posting

Customer service hiring is often high-volume, and many applications are screened by software before a person reads them. A clear, keyword-matched resume that shows the channels you support and the metrics you were measured on stands out.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

ATS filters look for support channels (phone, email, live chat, social), ticketing and CRM tools (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Gorgias), and specific skills such as de-escalation, order management, billing support or technical troubleshooting. Typing speed and languages spoken are worth listing when relevant.

Hiring managers look for the metrics contact centers use: customer satisfaction (CSAT), first contact resolution, average handle time, and quality scores. They also value reliability, schedule flexibility, and experience in the same industry, since retail, telecom, healthcare and software support all involve different knowledge.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • Customer support (phone, email, chat)
  • Zendesk / Freshdesk / Salesforce
  • Conflict resolution / de-escalation
  • CSAT
  • First contact resolution
  • Order and billing support
  • Troubleshooting
  • Data entry and accurate records
  • Multitasking in a fast-paced environment
  • Bilingual (if applicable)

Example bullet points

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Handled 60–80 phone and chat contacts per shift for an online retailer, maintaining a CSAT score above the team average for six consecutive months.
  • Example: Resolved escalated billing complaints and issued refunds within policy, reducing repeat contacts on the team's top issue.
  • Example: Wrote five help-center articles on return policies that the team now links in responses to common questions.

Common mistakes on customer service representative resumes

  • Using phrases like "people person" instead of concrete evidence.
  • Leaving out the tools and channels, which are often the main screening keywords.
  • Not mentioning metrics you were measured on, even if your results were solid.
