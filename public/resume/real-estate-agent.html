How to tailor a real estate agent resume to the job posting

Brokerages and teams recruit agents on production and process: how many transactions you close, where your business comes from and whether you can run a deal from contract to closing without hand-holding. Your license is the entry ticket; your numbers do the rest.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

Open with your license (state, year licensed, active status), whether you are a REALTOR® member, your MLS memberships and any designations: GRI, ABR, SRS, CRS, SRES, e-PRO. If you hold a broker's license, say so. Then state production in plain terms: closed transactions per year, sales volume, buyer/seller split and the price range or market area you serve.

Describe how you generate and manage business: sphere of influence, referrals, open houses, online leads (Zillow, Realtor.com), social media, farming, and the CRM you use (Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, LionDesk, Salesforce). Hiring brokers and team leaders also want to see transaction skills: comparative market analysis, pricing, listing presentations, negotiating offers and repairs, contract-to-close coordination, and tools such as DocuSign, dotloop or SkySlope.

If you are new, lean on sales, customer service or hospitality experience, local market knowledge and the brokerage training you have completed. Mention a team role (buyer's agent, showing agent, transaction coordinator) if that is what you did.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • Real estate license (state)
  • REALTOR® / MLS membership
  • Closed transactions and sales volume
  • Buyer and seller representation
  • Comparative market analysis (CMA)
  • Lead generation and CRM (Follow Up Boss, kvCORE)
  • Listing presentations
  • Contract negotiation
  • Contract-to-close coordination
  • DocuSign / dotloop / SkySlope

Example bullet points

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Closed 24 transactions totaling about $9M in volume in 2025, roughly 60% listings and 40% buyers, concentrated in the first-time-buyer segment of the metro area.
  • Example: Converted online leads from Zillow and the team CRM at a rate well above the team average by responding within five minutes and following a 10-touch follow-up plan.
  • Example: Worked as buyer's agent on a six-person team, running 15–20 showings a week and writing and negotiating offers through closing.

Common mistakes on real estate agent resumes

  • Leaving out transaction counts or volume, which is what brokers and teams actually evaluate.
  • Listing responsibilities ("helped clients buy homes") instead of outcomes and lead sources.
  • Not naming your market area, price range and CRM.
  • Claiming top-producer or award status without the year, brokerage or basis.
