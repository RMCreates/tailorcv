How to tailor a IT support specialist resume to the job posting

IT support hiring is about the environments you have supported and how well you resolve tickets. The resume should state the tier you worked, the user count, the platforms and tools you administered and your results on resolution and satisfaction.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

Say what level of support you provide (tier 1, tier 2, desktop support, help desk lead) and the scale: number of users, sites, devices and tickets per week. Name the environments: Windows and macOS, Microsoft 365 and Entra ID (Azure AD), Active Directory, Google Workspace, Intune, Jamf, VPN, printers, mobile devices and conferencing systems. Mention the ticketing system (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Zendesk, Freshservice) and remote support tools.

List certifications that postings filter on: CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+, Microsoft certifications (MD-102, AZ-900), ITIL Foundation, Apple or Google administrator certifications. Describe networking fundamentals you apply (DNS, DHCP, TCP/IP, Wi-Fi troubleshooting), imaging and deployment tools, hardware repair and asset management.

Managers care about outcomes: first-contact resolution rate, average response and resolution times, SLA compliance, user satisfaction scores, knowledge base articles written and projects such as device refreshes, onboarding automation or migrations. Customer service tone and clear written communication matter as much as technical range.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • Tier 1 / Tier 2 technical support
  • Ticketing (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Zendesk)
  • Windows and macOS troubleshooting
  • Microsoft 365 / Entra ID / Active Directory
  • Device management (Intune, Jamf)
  • Networking basics (DNS, DHCP, VPN, Wi-Fi)
  • Hardware setup, imaging and repair
  • CompTIA A+ / Network+ / Security+
  • Onboarding and account provisioning
  • SLA and first-contact resolution

Example bullet points

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Provided tier 1 and 2 support for 650 users across three offices, resolving about 90 tickets per week in ServiceNow with a first-contact resolution rate near 75%.
  • Example: Administered Microsoft 365, Entra ID and Intune for the company, automating onboarding so new hires received a configured laptop and accounts on day one.
  • Example: Led a 300-device laptop refresh over six weeks using Autopilot imaging, with no missed SLA on the related tickets.

Common mistakes on IT support specialist resumes

  • Not stating user count, ticket volume or tier, which define the scope of your experience.
  • Listing technologies without saying whether you administered them or only used them.
  • Leaving out metrics such as resolution rate and SLA performance.
  • Omitting certifications that the posting lists as required or preferred.
