How to tailor a recruiter resume to the job posting

Recruiting leaders hire recruiters the way they evaluate pipelines: with numbers. Hires per quarter, time-to-fill, requisition load, the functions you recruit for and the tools you source in matter far more than a list of soft skills.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

State the kind of recruiting you do: agency or in-house, full-cycle or sourcing-only, and the functions and levels you fill (software engineering, sales, healthcare, hourly/high-volume, executive). Then give the metrics hiring managers compare: requisitions carried at once, hires per month or year, average time-to-fill, offer acceptance rate, candidate pipeline conversion and, for agency work, placements and billings.

Describe your process: hiring manager intake meetings, writing job descriptions, sourcing with Boolean search and LinkedIn Recruiter, screening, structured interviews, compensation and offer negotiation, closing and onboarding handoff. Name the ATS and tools: Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, Ashby, Gem, SeekOut, HireEZ, and any scheduling or assessment platforms.

Employers also look for programs you have run or improved: employer branding, referral programs, university recruiting, diversity sourcing strategies, interviewer training and reporting on funnel metrics. If you built a function from scratch or reduced agency spend, say so with numbers.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • Full-cycle recruiting
  • Sourcing (LinkedIn Recruiter, Boolean)
  • ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, iCIMS)
  • Time-to-fill and hires per quarter
  • Hiring manager partnership
  • Offer negotiation and closing
  • Technical / sales / healthcare recruiting (specialty)
  • Candidate experience
  • Pipeline and funnel reporting
  • Employer branding and referral programs

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Carried 18–22 open requisitions across engineering and product, making 55 hires in a year with an average time-to-fill of 38 days and a 92% offer acceptance rate.
  • Example: Sourced passive candidates with Boolean search, LinkedIn Recruiter and Gem, generating roughly 40% of all engineering hires from outbound outreach.
  • Example: Built a structured interview and scorecard process in Greenhouse for the sales organization, reducing debrief time and improving hiring manager satisfaction scores.

Common mistakes on recruiter resumes

  • No numbers: recruiting is one of the easiest roles to quantify, and leaving metrics out looks like an omission.
  • Not saying which functions and levels you recruit, so the reader cannot judge domain fit.
  • Omitting your ATS and sourcing tools.
  • Describing yourself as a "people person" instead of showing process and outcomes.
