How to tailor an accountant resume to the job posting

Accounting resumes are screened carefully for credentials, standards and systems. A recruiter hiring for a general ledger role needs to see month-end close, reconciliations and the ERP they use; a public accounting firm looks for audit or tax experience and progress toward a CPA.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

List your credentials clearly: CPA (and state), CPA eligible, or the number of exam sections passed. ATS filters often screen on GAAP or IFRS, ERPs (NetSuite, SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks, Sage Intacct, Dynamics) and functions such as accounts payable, accounts receivable, general ledger, payroll, audit and tax.

Hiring managers look for accuracy, deadlines met, and process improvements: closing faster, reducing reconciling items, automating manual work, and supporting clean audits. Mention company size or revenue range and the number of entities you handled, because complexity varies a lot.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • CPA / CPA eligible
  • GAAP / IFRS
  • Month-end and year-end close
  • Account reconciliations
  • General ledger
  • Journal entries / accruals
  • NetSuite / SAP / QuickBooks
  • Financial statement preparation
  • Audit support
  • Advanced Excel

Example bullet points

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Managed month-end close for three legal entities on NetSuite, reducing the close from eight business days to five.
  • Example: Prepared reconciliations for 40 balance sheet accounts and cleared a backlog of aged reconciling items before the annual audit.
  • Example: Built an Excel model to automate the prepaid expense schedule, removing a manual calculation step each month.

Common mistakes on accountant resumes

  • Being vague about CPA status. Recruiters need to know exactly where you stand.
  • Not naming the ERP or accounting software you used.
  • Overstating responsibility, such as claiming you "owned" a close you supported.
