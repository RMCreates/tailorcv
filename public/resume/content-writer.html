How to tailor a content writer resume to the job posting

For content writers, the resume itself is a writing sample. Hiring managers will notice clear, specific, error-free writing, and they will notice flabby phrasing. Show the kinds of content you write, for whom, and what it achieved.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

ATS filters often look for content types (blog posts, long-form guides, case studies, email, landing pages, product descriptions, technical documentation), SEO skills (keyword research, on-page optimization), CMS platforms (WordPress, Webflow, HubSpot) and tools such as Google Search Console, Ahrefs or Semrush. Industry knowledge, for example B2B SaaS, finance or healthcare, often matters.

Hiring managers want links to published samples that match their content type, your output volume, how you work with subject-matter experts and editors, and results such as organic traffic, rankings, or conversions. Be truthful about which results came from your writing versus broader SEO work.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • SEO writing
  • Keyword research
  • Long-form content
  • Editing and proofreading
  • WordPress / Webflow CMS
  • Content strategy
  • Subject-matter expert interviews
  • Style guides
  • Google Search Console / Ahrefs / Semrush
  • B2B or industry-specific writing

Example bullet points

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Wrote 4–6 long-form SEO articles per month for a B2B payroll software blog, several of which became the site's top organic landing pages.
  • Example: Interviewed product managers and customers to write eight case studies used by the sales team.
  • Example: Created the company's editorial style guide and edited articles from three freelance writers.

Common mistakes on content writer resumes

  • No links to published samples.
  • Cliches like "passionate wordsmith" instead of specific work.
  • Typos or inconsistent formatting, which undercut the whole application.
