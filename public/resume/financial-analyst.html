How to tailor a financial analyst resume to the job posting

Financial analyst roles split into corporate FP&A, investment and banking analysis, and commercial or pricing analysis. Each uses different vocabulary, so your resume should speak the language of the posting you are applying to.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

For FP&A roles, recruiters and ATS filters look for budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, and tools such as Excel, Adaptive Insights, Anaplan, Hyperion or a BI tool. For investment roles, they look for financial modeling, valuation (DCF, comparables), due diligence, and credentials such as progress toward the CFA.

Hiring managers want evidence that your analysis influenced decisions: a budget you built, a forecast leaders relied on, a variance you explained, or a business case that was approved or rejected because of your work. Partnering with business leaders and presenting to executives is a key signal for FP&A roles.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • Financial modeling
  • Budgeting and forecasting
  • Variance analysis
  • Advanced Excel (INDEX/MATCH, pivot tables)
  • Anaplan / Adaptive / Hyperion
  • Valuation (DCF, comparables)
  • Business partnering
  • Management reporting
  • SQL or Power BI
  • CFA (if applicable)

Example bullet points

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Built the annual operating budget model for a $60M division, consolidating inputs from six department heads.
  • Example: Produced monthly budget-vs-actual reporting with commentary for the CFO, flagging a freight-cost trend that led to renegotiating a carrier contract.
  • Example: Developed a three-statement model and DCF for a potential acquisition, used by leadership in the go/no-go decision.

Common mistakes on financial analyst resumes

  • Using investment banking vocabulary for an FP&A role, or vice versa.
  • Listing "Excel" without saying how advanced, when modeling depth is the key filter.
  • Describing reports produced without saying who used them and for what.
