How to tailor a human resources manager resume to the job posting

HR manager roles can lean toward generalist work, employee relations, talent acquisition, or HR operations and systems. A strong resume makes your area of depth obvious while showing you understand the employment law and people risks that come with the job.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

ATS filters often look for HR functions (employee relations, recruitment, onboarding, performance management, compensation and benefits), HRIS platforms (Workday, BambooHR, ADP, UKG), and credentials such as SHRM-CP, SHRM-SCP, PHR or CIPD in the UK. Compliance terms matter too: FMLA, ADA, EEO, or local equivalents.

Hiring managers look for the size of the population you supported, the number of locations or countries, and outcomes such as reduced turnover, faster time-to-hire, improved engagement results, or policies you introduced. Show judgment in sensitive situations, but never include confidential details about individual employees.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • Employee relations
  • Performance management
  • Talent acquisition
  • Onboarding
  • Compensation and benefits
  • HRIS (Workday, BambooHR, ADP)
  • Employment law compliance
  • Policy development
  • SHRM-CP / PHR
  • Employee engagement

Example bullet points

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Served as HR business partner for 350 employees across two manufacturing sites, handling employee relations cases and investigations.
  • Example: Rolled out a new performance review process in BambooHR, training 30 managers and reaching full completion in the first cycle.
  • Example: Revised the employee handbook with outside counsel to reflect new state leave laws.

Common mistakes on human resources manager resumes

  • Listing HR functions without scale (employees supported, locations).
  • Sharing details of specific employee cases that should stay confidential.
  • Omitting HRIS experience, which is a common screening filter.
