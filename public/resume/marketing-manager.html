How to tailor a marketing manager resume to the job posting

Marketing manager roles range from brand and content to performance and lifecycle marketing. Recruiters want to see that your channel experience matches theirs and that you can tie campaigns to pipeline or revenue, not just impressions.

What recruiters and ATS filters look for

ATS filters typically scan for channels (paid search, paid social, SEO, email, events), platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, HubSpot, Marketo, Salesforce, GA4), and functions (demand generation, product marketing, brand). Use the exact names in the posting and make sure the channels it emphasizes appear in your most recent role.

Hiring managers look for budget ownership, team or agency management, and results expressed in the metrics their business uses: leads, MQLs, pipeline, CAC, ROAS, conversion rate or retention. Be clear about what you owned versus what you contributed to, especially in larger teams.

Keywords and skills to include (when they're true for you)

Only use the ones you can back up with real experience, and prefer the exact wording of the posting you're applying to.

  • Demand generation
  • Campaign strategy
  • Google Ads / Meta Ads
  • HubSpot / Marketo / Salesforce
  • SEO / content marketing
  • Email and lifecycle marketing
  • Marketing analytics / GA4
  • Budget management
  • Brand positioning
  • Agency and team management

Example bullet points

These are illustrative examples, not real candidates. Use them for structure (action, context, result) and replace the details and numbers with your own.

  • Example: Owned a $400k annual paid media budget across Google and LinkedIn, reallocating spend toward the two best-converting campaigns and lowering cost per qualified lead.
  • Example: Launched a five-email onboarding sequence in HubSpot that improved trial activation for new sign-ups.
  • Example: Managed two content marketers and an SEO agency; organic sign-ups became the second-largest acquisition channel within a year.

Common mistakes on marketing manager resumes

  • Reporting vanity metrics (impressions, followers) for a role measured on pipeline.
  • Listing every channel equally when the posting cares about two or three.
  • Forgetting budget size, which signals seniority more clearly than titles do.
